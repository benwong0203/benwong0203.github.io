繁體中文

本政策適用於 Ben Wong 發行的遊戲。之後如有新遊戲，也會使用本頁，網址維持不變。若某款遊戲的資料處理方式與此頁不同，會另作說明。

1. 開發者與聯絡方式

開發者為 Ben Wong。如對本政策或資料處理有疑問，請來信 benben3445@hotmail.com。

2. 遊戲在裝置上保存的資料

這些遊戲通常不需要帳號，也不需登入。進度、設定和其他遊戲資料一般都只存在你的裝置上。開發者沒有為這些遊戲營運玩家帳號伺服器或第一方分析服務，遊戲內也不會要求你提交姓名、電郵或支付卡，目前也沒有 App 內購。

3. 廣告

部分遊戲可能顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告，並可選擇觀看獎勵廣告，以換取遊戲內獎勵。

請求或顯示廣告時，Google 可能依照其政策，以及隨 App 附帶的 Google Mobile Ads SDK 隱私清單，收集和處理資料。該清單可能包括：裝置識別碼（並標示可用於廣告、開發者廣告或分析）、廣告資料、產品互動、粗略位置，以及效能、診斷或當機資料等。實際類別與用途以當時包裝內的 SDK 清單為準，SDK 更新後可能改變。

在需要同意訊息的地區，遊戲可先顯示 Google 官方表單，再請求廣告。若 SDK 表示需要隱私選項，也可在設定中開啟官方表單。

4. 追蹤

目前這些遊戲的正式設定沒有啟用 App Tracking Transparency 提示。Google Mobile Ads SDK 的隱私清單仍可能宣告裝置識別碼用於廣告相關用途。你也可以在裝置系統設定中管理與廣告相關的選項。

5. 權限

除非個別遊戲另有說明，否則遊戲不打算使用系統相機、麥克風或照片圖庫。若遊戲內有相簿功能，相關內容會存在遊戲自己的存檔裡。

6. 兒童

這些遊戲不是「兒童專用」(Made for Kids)，也不是以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。含有廣告的遊戲不應標示為兒童專用。

7. 分享與出售

開發者不會出售你的遊戲進度。為了提供和衡量廣告，廣告合作夥伴（Google）會按其政策處理上述廣告相關資料。

8. 你的選擇

在需要隱私訊息的地區，你可以透過 Google 官方表單完成或稍後更改選擇。刪除 App 即會刪除裝置上的遊戲存檔。

9. 變更

本政策如有修改，會更新文首日期，並以新文本取代本頁內容。本頁將繼續作為所有適用遊戲的共用隱私權政策網址。

English

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 (Hong Kong time, UTC+8).

This policy applies to games published by Ben Wong. Future games will use the same page, and the address will stay the same. If a particular game handles data differently, that will be described separately.

1. Developer and contact

The developer is Ben Wong. For questions about this policy or how data is handled, email benben3445@hotmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

These games usually do not require an account or sign-in. Progress, settings, and other play data are generally stored on your device. The developer does not operate a player account server or a first-party analytics service for these games, and the games do not ask you to submit a name, email, or payment card. There are currently no in-app purchases.

3. Advertising

Some games may show Google AdMob banner ads and optional rewarded ads for in-game rewards.

When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process data as described in Google's policies and in the Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy manifest bundled with the app. That manifest may include device ID (which may be flagged for advertising, developer advertising, or analytics), advertising data, product interaction, coarse location, and performance, diagnostic, or crash data. The exact categories and uses follow the SDK manifest shipped with the app and may change when the SDK is updated.

In regions where a message is required, the game can show Google's official form before ads are requested. If the SDK says a privacy-options entry is required, that official form can also be opened from Settings.

4. Tracking

These games are not currently configured to show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. The Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy manifest may still declare device ID for advertising-related purposes. You can also use device system settings that relate to advertising.

5. Permissions

Unless a particular game says otherwise, the games are not intended to use the system camera, microphone, or photo library. If a game includes an in-game album, that content is stored in the game's own save data.

6. Children

These games are not Made for Kids and are not directed at children under 13. Games that include ads should not be declared Made for Kids.

7. Sharing and sale

The developer does not sell your play progress. Advertising partners (Google) process advertising-related data described above in order to provide and measure ads, according to their policies.

8. Your choices

In regions where a privacy message is required, you can complete or later change those choices through Google's official form. Deleting the app removes the save data stored on the device.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and this page will be replaced with the new text. This page will remain the shared privacy policy address for the applicable games.
