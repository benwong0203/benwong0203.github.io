支援 隱私權政策

繁體中文

本政策適用於 Ben Wong 發行的遊戲。若某款遊戲的資料處理方式與此頁不同，會另作說明。

本政策按照 App Store 審核指引，說明：收集哪些資料、如何收集、全部用途；與第三方分享時的保護要求；以及資料保留、刪除與撤回同意的方法。

1. 開發者與聯絡方式

開發者為 Ben Wong。如對本政策、資料處理、撤回同意或要求刪除資料有疑問，請來信 benben3445@hotmail.com。支援頁為 https://benwong0203.github.io/。

2. 收集哪些資料、如何收集，以及全部用途

目前這些遊戲通常不需要帳號或登入，遊戲內也不會要求你提交姓名、電郵或支付卡。開發者沒有為這些遊戲營運玩家帳號伺服器或第一方分析服務，目前也沒有 App 內購。資料處理分為以下兩類。

2.1 儲存在你裝置上的遊戲資料

你遊玩時，遊戲會在裝置上建立和更新進度、設定、名稱、物品、遊戲內相簿或其他遊玩資料。這些資料由遊戲在裝置上直接產生和儲存，用途是讓遊戲在你的裝置上繼續運作，例如記住進度和設定。開發者不會把這些遊戲進度上傳到自己的伺服器，也不會用這些資料做廣告或分析。

2.2 廣告相關資料

部分遊戲可能顯示 Google AdMob 橫幅廣告，並可選擇觀看獎勵廣告，以換取遊戲內獎勵。當遊戲請求或顯示廣告時，會透過隨 App 附帶的 Google Mobile Ads SDK，向 Google 請求廣告。

Google 可能依照其政策及該 SDK 的隱私清單，收集和處理資料。該清單可能包括：裝置識別碼（並標示可用於廣告、開發者廣告或分析）、廣告資料、產品互動、粗略位置，以及效能、診斷或當機資料。這些資料由 Google 在提供、評估或改善廣告服務時收集和使用。實際類別與用途以當時包裝內的 SDK 清單為準，SDK 更新後可能改變。

在需要同意訊息的地區，遊戲可先顯示 Google 官方表單，再請求廣告。若 SDK 表示需要隱私選項，也可在設定中開啟官方表單。

目前這些遊戲的正式設定沒有啟用 App Tracking Transparency 提示。即使如此，Google Mobile Ads SDK 的隱私清單仍可能宣告裝置識別碼用於廣告相關用途。你也可以在裝置系統設定中管理與廣告或追蹤相關的選項。

3. 與第三方分享，以及同等保護

開發者不會出售你的遊戲進度，也不會把裝置上的遊戲進度分享給第三方。

為了提供和衡量廣告，遊戲會把請求廣告所需的資料交由廣告網絡及第三方 SDK 處理。目前這是指 Google（Google AdMob／Google Mobile Ads SDK）。開發者目前沒有母公司、子公司或其他關係企業會取得這些資料。

開發者確認：任何獲分享使用者資料的第三方（例如分析工具、廣告網絡及第三方 SDK，以及任何會取得使用者資料的母公司、子公司或其他關係企業）會提供與本政策所述、以及 App Store 審核指引所要求相同或同等的使用者資料保護。目前適用的第三方為 Google，其資料處理亦受 Google 隱私權政策 及 Google 廣告資訊 約束。

4. 資料保留、刪除與撤回同意

4.1 保留

儲存在裝置上的遊戲資料會保留在該裝置，直到你刪除 App、清除該 App 的資料，或自行重置進度。廣告相關資料由 Google 按其政策保留；開發者並不操作 Google 的保留期限。

4.2 刪除

刪除 App，或在系統設定中清除該 App 的資料，即會刪除裝置上由該遊戲保存的資料。由於開發者通常沒有在伺服器保存你的遊戲進度副本，刪除裝置上的 App 資料後，開發者一般沒有另一份進度可再刪除。

至於 Google 為提供廣告而處理的資料，你可依照 Google 的隱私權政策與工具提出刪除或其他權利請求。你也可以寫信到 benben3445@hotmail.com 要求刪除資料，或查詢如何提出該請求。來信請說明涉及的遊戲和裝置，開發者會在合理範圍內協助處理，或指引你向相關第三方提出請求。

4.3 撤回同意

在需要隱私訊息或同意的地區，你可以透過遊戲內的 Google 官方表單完成、更改或撤回選擇。若 SDK 提供隱私選項，也可在遊戲設定中再開啟該表單。你也可以在裝置的系統設定中管理與廣告、追蹤或隱私相關的選項。若日後遊戲顯示 App Tracking Transparency 提示，你可在系統提示或系統設定中更改選擇。

撤回廣告相關同意後，部分遊戲可能仍會顯示較少依據個人資料的廣告，或在未能請求廣告時不顯示廣告。這不會刪除已保存在裝置上的遊戲進度。

5. 權限

除非個別遊戲另有說明，否則遊戲不打算使用系統相機、麥克風或照片圖庫。若遊戲內有相簿功能，相關內容會存在遊戲自己的存檔裡，而不是系統照片圖庫。

6. 兒童

這些遊戲不是「兒童專用」(Made for Kids)，也不是以 13 歲以下兒童為對象。含有廣告的遊戲不應標示為兒童專用。

7. 變更

本政策如有修改，會更新文首日期，並以新文本取代本頁內容。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 (Hong Kong time, UTC+8).

This policy applies to games published by Ben Wong. If a particular game handles data differently, that will be described separately.

This policy is written to identify what data, if any, is collected, how it is collected, and all uses of that data; to confirm the protection required when data is shared with third parties; and to explain retention, deletion, and how you can revoke consent or request deletion.

1. Developer and contact

The developer is Ben Wong. For questions about this policy, data handling, withdrawing consent, or requesting deletion, email benben3445@hotmail.com. The support page is https://benwong0203.github.io/.

2. What data is collected, how it is collected, and all uses of that data

These games usually do not require an account or sign-in, and they do not ask you to submit a name, email, or payment card. The developer does not operate a player account server or a first-party analytics service for these games, and there are currently no in-app purchases. Data is handled in the two ways below.

2.1 Game data stored on your device

When you play, the game creates and updates progress, settings, names, inventory, an in-game album, or other play data on your device. This data is created and stored directly on the device by the game. It is used only to operate the game on your device, for example to remember your progress and settings. The developer does not upload this play progress to a server of their own, and does not use this data for advertising or analytics.

2.2 Advertising-related data

Some games may show Google AdMob banner ads and optional rewarded ads for in-game rewards. When a game requests or shows ads, it uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK bundled with the app to request ads from Google.

Google may collect and process data as described in Google's policies and in that SDK's privacy manifest. The manifest may include device ID (which may be flagged for advertising, developer advertising, or analytics), advertising data, product interaction, coarse location, and performance, diagnostic, or crash data. Google collects and uses this data to provide, measure, or improve advertising services. The exact categories and uses follow the SDK manifest shipped with the app and may change when the SDK is updated.

In regions where a message is required, the game can show Google's official form before ads are requested. If the SDK says a privacy-options entry is required, that official form can also be opened from Settings.

These games are not currently configured to show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Even so, the Google Mobile Ads SDK privacy manifest may still declare device ID for advertising-related purposes. You can also use device system settings that relate to advertising or tracking.

3. Sharing with third parties, and equal protection

The developer does not sell your play progress and does not share on-device play progress with third parties.

To provide and measure ads, data needed to request ads is handled by an advertising network and third-party SDK. At present, that is Google (Google AdMob / the Google Mobile Ads SDK). The developer currently has no parent, subsidiary, or other related entity that will have access to this data.

The developer confirms that any third party with whom an app shares user data — such as analytics tools, advertising networks, and third-party SDKs, as well as any parent, subsidiary, or other related entity that will have access to user data — will provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this privacy policy and required by the App Store Review Guidelines. The third party currently involved is Google, whose handling of data is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

4. Retention, deletion, and withdrawing consent

4.1 Retention

Game data stored on your device is kept on that device until you delete the app, clear the app's data, or reset your progress yourself. Advertising-related data is retained by Google according to Google's policies. The developer does not control Google's retention periods.

4.2 Deletion

Deleting the app, or clearing the app's data in system settings, deletes the game data stored on the device. Because the developer typically does not keep a copy of your play progress on a server, there is usually no additional copy for the developer to delete after the on-device data is removed.

For data that Google processes in order to provide ads, you may use the tools and rights described in Google's privacy policy to request deletion or exercise other rights. You may also email benben3445@hotmail.com to request deletion or to ask how to make that request. Please include the relevant game and device. The developer will help where reasonably possible, or will direct you to the relevant third party.

4.3 Withdrawing consent

In regions where a privacy message or consent is required, you can complete, change, or withdraw your choices through Google's official form in the game. If the SDK provides a privacy-options entry, you can also open that form again from the game's settings. You can also use your device's system settings that relate to advertising, tracking, or privacy. If a game later shows an App Tracking Transparency prompt, you can change that choice in the system prompt or in system settings.

After you withdraw advertising-related consent, some games may still show ads that rely less on personal data, or may show no ad if one cannot be requested. This does not delete play progress already stored on the device.

5. Permissions

Unless a particular game says otherwise, the games are not intended to use the system camera, microphone, or photo library. If a game includes an in-game album, that content is stored in the game's own save data, not in the system photo library.

6. Children

These games are not Made for Kids and are not directed at children under 13. Games that include ads should not be declared Made for Kids.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated and this page will be replaced with the new text.
